Marks both kinds of answers

Multiple choice

Each answer is checked against the teacher's key. A right answer earns the full marks for that question.

Written answers

The answer is compared with the teacher's ideal answer, and keywords add a bonus. Partial credit is possible, and the student gets a line of feedback. If the grader can't judge an answer, it is flagged for manual review.

Finds weak topics

A topic is flagged when a student gets half or more of its questions wrong. Each weak topic comes with a revision resource, and teachers see every student who scored below 70%.

Proctored in the browser, with three strikes

Students must allow their camera and microphone, and the exam runs in fullscreen. Leaving fullscreen, switching tabs or windows, and copy, paste or right-click attempts are blocked or logged with a time and reason. The third violation cancels the exam.

These are browser-based checks. They discourage and record, but they can't catch everything, so teachers review the log.

Four-strike rule

Examples of violations

  • Switch tab
  • Exit fullscreen
  • Paste text
  • Not face detected

Four proctoring violations cancel the exam. The teacher is notified and can review the violation log.

How an exam runs

  1. The teacher builds the exam

    Add questions, marks, topics and answer keys, then publish it for a department.

  2. The student sits it

    They read the instructions, allow camera and microphone, and answer in fullscreen.

  3. SmartEdu marks it

    When the exam is submitted, every answer is scored and the student sees feedback on each one.

  4. Everyone sees what's next

    Students get their weak topics and revision links. Teachers see who needs help.
